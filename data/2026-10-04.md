### [1] [HistoRAG: A Citation-Grounded Question Answering Assistant for Teaching with Scanned Local History and Heritage Archives](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29184)
*Hongzhou Duan,Zikun Guo*

Main category: cs.SE

TL;DR: HistoRAG 是一款专为历史教学设计的问答助手，它能精准解析扫描版历史文献，并提供精确到页码的来源引用，有效提升了教学材料的使用效率与可信度。

Motivation: 历史教学资源多为无文本层的扫描件或行政表格，现有的通用聊天机器人虽然流畅但缺乏可验证来源，无法满足教师教学的严谨需求。

Method: 1. 使用视觉语言模型（VLM）将扫描件转录为带置信度的文本；2. 构建混合索引、关系数据库和知识图谱三种存储库；3. 设计轻量级路由器，根据问题类型分发至对应的存储库；4. 通过聊天界面提供带有页码引用的回答，确保可验证性。

Result: 在包含36卷扫描件和相关目录的基准测试集上，HistoRAG 在回答准确率上优于传统的段落检索和基于图的检索系统，且单次问答成本更低。

Conclusion: HistoRAG 通过结合多源检索（文本、关系库、知识图谱）和来源溯源机制，有效解决了历史文化教学资料在数字化利用中的难点，实现了高准确率且可验证的问答，同时降低了计算成本。

Abstract: Teachers who prepare lessons on local history and cultural heritage work from material that is hard to use. The primary sources are scanned books without a text layer, and the supporting records are administrative catalogs released as spreadsheets. A general chatbot answers such questions fluently but without a verifiable source, which is the property a teacher needs most. This paper presents HistoRAG, a question answering assistant that answers from one regional collection and cites a volume and a page for every fact. HistoRAG transcribes each page with a vision language model and keeps a line level confidence from the token probabilities. It builds three stores from the same collection: a hybrid text index, a relational catalog database, and a knowledge graph extracted only from entity dense passages. A lightweight router sends each question to the stores it needs, so that counting questions reach the database and relational questions reach the graph. We build a benchmark of 516 questions over a collection of 36 scanned volumes and the heritage catalogs of the same region, covering statistical, factual, temporal, and multi-hop questions. HistoRAG answers more questions correctly than passage retrieval baselines and than a graph based retrieval system, at a far smaller cost per question. The assistant runs behind a chat interface, so a teacher can check any statement against the page it came from.
