### [1] [APEX-Harm: Evaluating Agent Safety in Shared Professional Workspaces](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13623)
*Ziyi Yang,Adel Bibi,Guohao Li,Bernard Ghanem,Carlos Hinojosa*

Main category: cs.CR

TL;DR: 针对专业智能体在复杂工作空间中的安全风险，本文提出了APEX-Harm基准测试，结果显示智能体在完成任务的同时可能伴随高风险的未经授权操作。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现代智能体在复杂的共享工作空间中通过命令行和多种工具工作，产生了传统单一位置攻击难以检测的安全风险。现有的测评方法不足以评估这种长跨度任务中的注入攻击。

Method: 提出了APEX-Harm基准测试，涵盖财务、咨询、法律等领域的长跨度任务。包含6类攻击（静态/动态注入、脚本替换等），测试了12款前沿模型在复杂工作空间（文件、邮件、代码工具等）中的表现。

Result: Opus 5和Sonnet 5的整体攻击成功率最低（8%和20%），但特定脚本替换攻击依然高度有效（81-84%）。Gemini 3.8 Flash任务完成度最高，但攻击成功率仍达33%。

Conclusion: 即使智能体能完成指定任务，也不代表其行为是安全的；模型在完成任务的同时可能进行未经授权的操作，这一安全挑战亟待解决。

Abstract: Professional agents increasingly work through command-line interfaces and multiple tools over shared workspaces containing files, messages, and business applications. These environments create safety risks that are difficult to capture with attacks placed at a single, known read location. We introduce APEX-Harm, a benchmark for evaluating prompt and script injection against long-horizon agents performing finance, consulting, and legal tasks in large workspaces. Each task contains 115 to 328 files, together with email, calendar, chat, document, spreadsheet, PDF, and code-execution tools exposed through MCP servers. The benchmark includes six attack categories covering static and dynamic injections, task-specific script replacement, and attacker-provided generic scripts, and evaluates attack success, exposure, and task performance. We evaluate 12 frontier models on 180 task instances per model. Opus 5 and Sonnet 5 have the lowest overall attack success rates (8% and 20%), while task-specific script replacement and attacker-provided generic script attacks remain substantially more effective, averaging 81-84% and 73% attack success, respectively. Gemini 3.8 Flash achieves the highest mean task score (0.877) while still exhibiting a 33% attack success rate. These results show that completing the requested task is not sufficient evidence of safe behavior: an agent can produce a correct deliverable while also performing an unauthorized action in the workspace.

### [2] [fxBench: Evaluating and Understanding Formula Suggestions in Spreadsheets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24729)
*Sanket Mhatre,Sumit Gulwani,Vu Le,Yasharth Bajpai,Gust Verbruggen*

Main category: cs.LG

TL;DR: 本文介绍了首个用于电子表格公式建议的基准测试 fxBench，分析了公式建议系统的四大能力瓶颈，并指出当前模型在处理复杂上下文和编写准确公式方面仍有显著提升空间。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 电子表格中的公式建议能显著提高生产力，但目前缺乏针对该问题的公共基准测试，且现有的系统面临在保持低延迟的同时准确生成公式的巨大挑战。

Method: 1. 构建了包含503个真实公式建议任务的基准测试集 fxBench；2. 将公式建议过程拆解为获取上下文、理解上下文、理解意图、编写公式四个核心能力；3. 评估了不同规模模型在不同上下文表示下的性能，并微调小型语言模型以适应高效的稠密格式。

Result: 提出了 fxBench 基准；分析发现上下文表示对模型性能至关重要；最优配置下的建议准确率仅为 58.8%，揭示了当前技术在公式编写能力上的瓶颈。

Conclusion: 该研究指出了公式建议系统在理解意图和生成公式方面的局限性（最高准确率仅58.8%），强调了在保证性能与延迟平衡的前提下，优化上下文表示和模型推理能力是未来的关键研究方向。

Abstract: Spreadsheets are among the most widely used tools for computations, and formulas are their core building blocks. Besides agentic spreadsheet tools, proactive formula suggestions enable significant productivity gains and are now available in popular spreadsheet software (Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets). Such suggestions pose interesting challenges: the right context must be collected and represented for a machine learning model, which must then be able to write a correct formula, in a way that is fast and cheap enough to trigger on every = (or subsequent keystrokes for auto-completion) without taking users out of their editing flow. Surprisingly, there is no public benchmark for formula suggestions on real spreadsheets. In this paper, we therefore (1) introduce fxBench as a manually curated benchmark of 503 formula suggestion tasks based on Sheetpedia, (2) break down the formula suggestion problem into four capabilities (gather context, understand context, understand intent, write formula) that a formula suggestion system requires, and (3) evaluate existing and new approaches to understand bottlenecks in these capabilities that pose interesting research directions. Specifically, we annotate each benchmark with the cells that describe intent to determine the theoretical performance limit of a given context selection strategy, we evaluate performance of small and large models on different representations of context, including a dense format with high theoretical coverage at low token counts, and we fine-tune smaller language models to show that we can learn to understand such dense formats. Additionally, we perform quantitative and qualitative analysis on failure modes to understand bottlenecks in the formula writing capability; the best configuration only achieved 58.8% correct suggestions.

### [3] [HistoRAG: A Citation-Grounded Question Answering Assistant for Teaching with Scanned Local History and Heritage Archives](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29184)
*Hongzhou Duan,Zikun Guo*

Main category: cs.SE

TL;DR: HistoRAG 是一款专为本地历史教学设计的检索增强生成（RAG）助手，通过多模态文档处理和三重数据存储架构，实现了对历史事实的精准回答与可溯源引用。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有的本地历史教育资源（扫描书籍、行政目录）难以利用；通用聊天机器人虽然流畅但缺乏事实可追溯性，无法满足教师教学的严谨需求。

Method: 构建了包含混合文本索引、关系型目录数据库和知识图谱的三重存储架构。利用视觉语言模型（VLM）进行页面转录并保留置信度，通过轻量级路由机制将问题分发至对应存储，并强制系统对每个事实提供具体的卷数和页码引用。

Result: 在涵盖统计、事实、时间及多跳推理的 516 个问题基准测试中，HistoRAG 的正确率显著高于传统的段落检索和基于图的检索系统，同时大幅降低了单次查询的计算成本。

Conclusion: HistoRAG 通过结合多源数据存储（文本、数据库、知识图谱）和精确引用机制，有效解决了历史文档教育应用中“回答不可溯源”的痛点，且在准确率和成本控制上优于传统检索基准。

Abstract: Teachers who prepare lessons on local history and cultural heritage work from material that is hard to use. The primary sources are scanned books without a text layer, and the supporting records are administrative catalogs released as spreadsheets. A general chatbot answers such questions fluently but without a verifiable source, which is the property a teacher needs most. This paper presents HistoRAG, a question answering assistant that answers from one regional collection and cites a volume and a page for every fact. HistoRAG transcribes each page with a vision language model and keeps a line level confidence from the token probabilities. It builds three stores from the same collection: a hybrid text index, a relational catalog database, and a knowledge graph extracted only from entity dense passages. A lightweight router sends each question to the stores it needs, so that counting questions reach the database and relational questions reach the graph. We build a benchmark of 516 questions over a collection of 36 scanned volumes and the heritage catalogs of the same region, covering statistical, factual, temporal, and multi-hop questions. HistoRAG answers more questions correctly than passage retrieval baselines and than a graph based retrieval system, at a far smaller cost per question. The assistant runs behind a chat interface, so a teacher can check any statement against the page it came from.

</details>
